QuickSend Messenger

Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 28, 2026

About the extension

QuickSend Messenger is a Chrome extension (Manifest V3) that sends WhatsApp text messages from the browser toolbar using phone numbers you link with a QR code. You can create up to 5 profiles, each with its own linked number.

This page explains what data the extension handles, where it goes, and what it does not do.

Data the extension handles

  • Account username and password: used only to sign in. The password is never stored.
  • Session token: kept in the browser's extension storage to keep you signed in, and removed at logout.
  • Profiles: profile name, linked phone number, and linked status, kept in the browser's extension storage on your device.
  • Recipient phone number and message text: sent only when you press Send. They are not stored by the extension.

Where data goes

Data is sent only to the messaging service the extension connects to (the single host listed in its manifest), to sign in, link a number by QR code, check the link status, and send messages.

What the extension does not do

  • It does not read your WhatsApp chats or contacts, or your browsing history.
  • It does not use analytics, tracking, ads, or cookies.
  • It does not sell or share your data with third parties.
  • It does not use your data for any purpose other than sending your messages.
  • It does not run remote code.

Permissions

  • storage: to keep your session and profiles.
  • Host access to one messaging-service host only.

Data retention and deletion

Local data stays on your device until you log out (session) or uninstall the extension (everything). To delete server-side account data, contact us using the address below.

Children

The extension is not directed at children under 13.
